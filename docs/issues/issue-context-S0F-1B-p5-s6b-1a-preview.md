## Context

- The first deliverable of S6B-1A is a repo-level evidence total table that structures current evidence surfaces by family instead of relying on informal impressions.
- This initial version focuses on family-level inventory capturing the type, location, creator, retention intent, and access path of major evidence families.
- The inventory incorporates six taxonomy categories including human-ledger, fact-source, retained-summary, workflow-derived, tmp-scratch, and evidence-lite.
- The table serves as a concrete baseline for subsequent retention policies, generator policies, and migration sequencing in the S6B epic.
